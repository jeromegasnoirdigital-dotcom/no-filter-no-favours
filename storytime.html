No Filter, No Favours · Segment concept

Story Time, animated.

Rock tells the story in the chair. We animate it. His real audio, illustrated scene by scene, so the best stories travel as their own clips. Every story has a lesson.

How it works

01
Record

Rock tells the story on the episode, same as always.

02
Lock

The editor locks the story cut and hands off the audio.

03
Animate

The animator illustrates it over Rock's real voice.

04
Release

It plays as a cutaway in the episode and posts as its own short.
