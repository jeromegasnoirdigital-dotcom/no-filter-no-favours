About the world.

A conversation show hosted by Rock Da House: Toronto DJ, barbershop owner, twenty years in the city's music and nightlife. However crude the topic gets, it carries a lesson by the end. The humour is the delivery, not the point.

Shot in a working barbershop, kitchen and 100-person event space downtown. The room is part of the show, and the businesses in it show up on set with no sales pitch on camera.

Not a Toronto industry show. Not a rap-politics show. Toronto is where it's made, not what it's about.

In the chair

Doctors, tradespeople, a special-effects artist out of Regent Park, a working Detroit actor, a stunt performer. The barber chair is the story engine: every walk of life already sits in it, every week.
